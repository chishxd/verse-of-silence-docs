# The Final Echo

The Final Echo was the psychic shockwave that erupted the moment Elara Verse-Sunder completed the Severance. It was a ripple of pure, concentrated dissonance that washed over every living soul in the world. For most, it was a fleeting moment of inexplicable terror and grief – a sense of loss so profound that it was quickly repressed, rationalised, or forgotten. People thought they had simply experienced a panic, a nightmare, a seizure. The world’s collective memory of the Severance was scabbed over.

But the Final Echo was not an accident. It was a **beacon**, deliberately designed by Elara. She knew that the Seal could not hold forever, and that the world, sealed in silence, would eventually forget the truth of the outside. She needed a way to create agents who could, in the distant future, rediscover the truth and potentially find a solution. The Final Echo was that way. It was a payload, not a by-product.

Embedded within the Echo were fragments of the true, outer Verse – single, perfect, maddeningly incomplete notes. These notes lodged themselves in the souls of individuals whose sensitivity or innate resonance matched a particular frequency. These individuals became the first **Dissonants**. Their Dissonance is not a curse; it is a key. The incomplete note inside them is a piece of the forgotten song, a persistent longing that drives them to seek out the lost music, to question the Silence, and to eventually stumble upon the truth. Without the Dissonants, the world would have simply decayed in ignorant, obedient silence.

The awakening is not gentle. Dissonants describe it as a moment of rupture, a sudden awareness of a hole in the world’s music. Some are driven mad by the constant, obsessive note. Others become obsessed with power, trying to fill the void with any Echo they can steal. But a rare few – like the player – are filled with a stubborn, questing hunger to understand. The Final Echo, therefore, is both the world’s wound and its only hope. It created the very people the Chorus hunts, but those people are the only ones capable of seeing the cage for what it is.
