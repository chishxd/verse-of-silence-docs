# The Revolutionaries

The Revolutionaries are a scattered faction of Dissonants who believe they are freedom fighters. They are the player’s first allies, the ones who provide shelter, training, and the framing of the quest. They are not villains; they are sincere, passionate, and wrong.

## Key NPCs (to be developed)
- **The Captain:** A grizzled, older Dissonant who leads the cell. She is a true believer in the cause, but harbours private doubts about the more extreme members.
- **The Scholar:** A young, idealistic researcher who has uncovered fragments of pre‑Severance lore that don’t quite fit the official story. She is the first to voice questions.
- **The Hothead:** A Dissonant consumed by rage at the Chorus. He advocates for violent action and is a subtle lure toward the Power path.

## Arc
The Revolutionaries provide the tutorial and the early quests. As the story progresses and the truth about the Severance and the Hunger emerges, they must face the fact that their entire cause is based on a misunderstanding. Some refuse to accept it and become antagonists (especially on the Power path). Others, like the Scholar, become valuable allies on the Harmony path, joining the final Gathering of Voices with a note of humbled acceptance.
