# Shard Types

Echo shards are the physical, collectible pieces of resonance that fuel the Echo Weaving system. Each has a colour, a sound, and a thematic weight.

| Shard | Colour | Sound | Harmony Source | Power Source | Nature |
|-------|--------|-------|----------------|--------------|--------|
| **Water** | Blue, shimmering | Trickling, soft | Puddles, rain, befriended Glimmerkin | Rare, must be stolen from neutral sources | Flow, calm, sleep |
| **Light** | Gold, radiant | Faint choir hum | Sunbeams, crystals, solving puzzles | Tainted Light from killed Wardens or purified places | Revelation, healing, purity |
| **Stone** | Grey‑brown, solid | Low, grinding hum | Rock formations, natural barriers | Shattered constructs, collapsed ruins | Stability, defence, weight |
| **Fire** | Orange‑red, flickering | Crackling, hot | Volcanic vents, rare passion echoes | Killing Glimmerkin, burning living things, or from enemies | Destruction, passion, transformation |
| **Agony** | Sickly purple‑black, static‑edged | Screaming, distorted | *Never* naturally pure | Dropped by slain innocents, fallen Dissonants, or violently torn Echoes | Raw, addictive, deafening power |

**Taint and Purity:**
- Shards obtained through violence or from corrupted sources are **tainted**. They appear visually distorted (flickering, dark edges) and sound harsh. Using tainted shards contributes to **Resonance Drowning** (see [[Hollowing Mechanic]]).
- Shards obtained peacefully or from the environment are **pure**. They produce clean, predictable spells and do not affect the player’s inner song.

**Memory Motes** are special, non‑inventory shards that only appear during Pillar boss fights. They are tiny, glowing fragments of the Pillar’s original pure note. Collecting them fills the **Resonance Bar** and is essential for the Harmony path pacification.
