The psychic shockwave released when Elara severed the world. It was not an accident – it was a **beacon** designed to embed a fragment of the outer song into sensitive souls, creating Dissonants across the ages. These Dissonants are potential successors who might one day find a way to safely return the world to the Verse, or outlast the Hunger.
