# Demon of Sunder

The Demon of Sunder is the dark mentor of the Power path. It is a parasitic Dissonant, one of the “Mad” who found a way not just to survive, but to thrive by feeding on the Echoes of others. It presents itself as a grinning, charismatic guide to the player who chooses violence — and its ultimate goal is to consume them when they are fat with stolen power.

## Appearance & Voice
The Demon has no fixed form. It appears as a shifting, dark silhouette with too‑wide eyes and a constant, amused smile. Its voice is warm, reasonable, and laced with dark humour. It never yells. It never threatens. It simply offers easier paths, quicker power, and the thrill of dominance. It calls the player “friend” and “prodigy,” and it always sounds like it means it.

## Methods
The Demon approaches the player after their first acts of violence, offering “quests” that lower inhibition: “That village harbours Chorus spies. Burn it. Take their Echoes.” It provides tainted shards, reveals shortcuts to boss fights, and reframes every cruelty as strength. It never forces the player’s hand; it simply makes violence the most convenient, rewarding option.

## The True Goal
The Demon is a survivor of the earliest ages of Dissonance, driven mad by the Hunger’s whispers bleeding through the cracks. It has learned to feed on the Echoes of other Dissonants, and it sees the player as its finest project: a vessel to gather all the Pillars’ power, which it will then consume in one glorious, final betrayal. It wants to become the sole singer in the dying world, a god of screaming silence.

## Role in the Endings
- **Verse of Destruction:** The Demon either betrays the player at the climax (becoming the true final boss) or is defeated by the player, who then becomes the very monster the Demon wished to create.
- **Verse of Atonement:** When the player turns away from power, the Demon attacks in fury, attempting to rip the stolen Echoes back before the player can surrender them. Defeating the Demon here is the first step of the Redemption path. As it dies, it brands the player with the **Mark of Dissonance**, a curse that ensures the player’s soul will eventually unravel — a final spiteful gift.
