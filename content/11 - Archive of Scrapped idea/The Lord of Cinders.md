# The Lord of Cinders

The Lord of Cinders is the Pillar of **Rage**. He is a walking inferno, a charred silhouette of a man whose entire existence is a scream against an injustice that happened so long ago no one else remembers it.

## The Wound
The Lord was once a protector of a small community of Dissonants, a village hidden from the Chorus. When the Chorus found them, they did not just kill his people; they **silenced** them with the Echo of Stasis, permanently erasing their Echo‑binding and ensuring no trace of their songs remained. The Lord alone survived, his body burned beyond recognition, his soul fused with the agony of that moment. He has burned ever since, his rage so hot it has deafened him to everything else.

## The Inner Sanctum
His Sanctum is a red, smoke‑choked battlefield where ghostly flames in the shapes of his lost people flicker and scream. The player must navigate burning trenches and ruined barricades. Discordant Aspects:
- **Aspect of Vengeance:** A towering, flaming construct that mirrors the Lord’s own fury, attacking relentlessly.
- **Aspect of Grief:** A small, huddled figure of a child, glowing with dying embers, that cries silently.

At the Silenced Heart, the Lord’s pure note is a low, warm hum — the sound of a hearth‑fire in a safe home. When freed, his flames do not vanish, but they soften into a gentle, golden glow. He weeps tears of fire and either dissipates into a warm breeze or joins the player as a quiet, smouldering presence.
