The player awakens as a Dissonant, hunted by the Chorus. Rescued by Revolutionaries who preach the "Elara as liberator" myth. The player is taught to weave Echoes and sets out to gather the Sundered Verses.

**Key events:**
- Early encounter with a Glimmerkin – first moral choice (spare or kill).
- The world confirms the Revolutionaries' narrative: oppressive Chorus, hostile Wardens.
- If the player kills freely, they attract the **Demon of Sunder**.
- If they spare, the first Pillar acts as a Tree Sentinel, encouraging exploration.
