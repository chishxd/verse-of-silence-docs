The player enters the wider world, confronting the **Pillars of Dissonance**.

**Harmony Path:**
- Meets the **Saint of Echoes**, learns **Resonant Mirroring**.
- Enters Pillars' Inner Sanctums, fights their Discordant Aspects, restores colors, and liberates their pure notes.
- The world becomes more vibrant; allies are gained.

**Power Path:**
- Guided by the **Demon of Sunder**, kills Pillars and rips out their Echoes.
- Grows a hole in the chest; power increases but perception deafens.
- The world desaturates, NPCs flee.

**Throughout:** Cracks appear in the Revolutionaries' story. Ancient texts speak of severance. Wardens murmur truths. The Chorus's fear is glimpsed.
