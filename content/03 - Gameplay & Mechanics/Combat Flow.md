# Combat Flow

Combat in *The Verse of Silence* is a **close‑range, rhythm‑based dance**. It is not a bullet‑hell; it is a conversation where the player listens to enemy patterns, dodges through attacks, and seizes openings to cast spells or collect memory motes. The feel should be closer to **Sekiro** (pattern recognition, deflection timing) or **Hyper Light Drifter** (dash, slash, move) than to a twin‑stick shooter.

## The Player’s Verbs

1. **Sword Strike (Basic Melee)**
   - Fast, weak, always available.
   - Does minimal damage but builds a hidden **Pressure** (stagger) meter on enemies.
   - Can be used to interrupt slow attacks or finish a staggered foe.
   - On the Harmony path, using the sword too often is discouraged (it subtly shifts the moral tracker). On the Power path, it becomes a secondary tool to save shards.

2. **Dash / Roll**
   - Press a dedicated button (Shift / A on controller) while moving.
   - Short burst of speed with **i‑frames** in the middle of the animation.
   - Essential for dodging through boss attacks, escaping AoE, and repositioning.
   - The dash leaves a cosmetic trail: faint musical notes on Harmony, jagged dissonance on Power.

3. **Echo Weaving (Spells)**
   - Hold Weave button to slow time slightly and open the Weaving Wheel.
   - Select Lead and Echo shards, release to cast.
   - Spells have a **wind‑up** (a brief casting animation where the player is vulnerable) and a **cooldown** (the resonance ring must fade).
   - Most spells are **melee‑range bursts, short‑range cones, or zone‑control effects**. Only a few (like Molten Spike) are long‑range, and they are slow.

4. **Listen / Pacify (Harmony Path)**
   - Near a pacifiable enemy (Glimmerkin, a Warden with a cracked resonance), a prompt appears.
   - Hold the Listen button to enter a rhythm minigame: a circle pulses in time with the enemy’s hidden note. Tap in sync to fill a pacification meter. Full meter = enemy becomes non‑hostile or reveals a secret.
   - In boss fights, this is replaced by collecting Memory Motes.

## The Enemy’s Dance

Enemies, especially bosses, attack in **tells and sequences**. Example:
- A Warden raises its arm (tell) → slams the ground (attack) → creates a shockwave (AoE). The player must dash *through* the shockwave at the right moment, then punish during the Warden’s recovery.
- Bosses have **openings** after certain attacks. During an opening, the player can safely cast a spell, strike with the sword, or (on Harmony) collect Memory Motes that spawn.

The goal is not to whittle down a health bar from a distance, but to **learn the rhythm**, stay close, and use the correct response to each phrase of the song.
