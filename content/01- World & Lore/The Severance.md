
# The Severance

The Severance was the single most important act in the history of the world. It was a ritual of immeasurable complexity and cost, performed by Elara Verse-Sunder at the height of her power. Understanding it is key to understanding the entire setting.

Before the Severance, the world existed naturally within the Verse of Universe. It was a normal, evolving melody, subject to the cycle of dissolution and rebirth. Elara discovered that this dissolution was imminent and that the Hunger would consume the world’s song. The Severance was her solution: she would sever the metaphysical connection between her world and the greater Verse, wrapping the entire reality in a bubble of **self-referential Silence**. This bubble was not a physical wall, but a resonance field – a closed loop of Echoes that fed into themselves, mimicking a complete song. The world would hear only itself, and the outside would hear nothing.

To do this, Elara needed a power source that could sustain the bubble for eons. That power source was her own soul. She poured her consciousness, her very existence, into the Seal. She became the anchor, the immovable centre of the Silence. The act was fatal to her as a person, but her will persisted as a kind of living law, a constant hum that kept the edges of the bubble rigid.

**The Consequences:**
- **Salvation from the Hunger:** The Hunger could no longer detect or reach the world. It was temporarily safe.
- **The Silence:** The world was cut off from the life-giving, evolving music of the Verse. It became a closed system, a caged song that could only repeat its existing notes. Magic dwindled. Life grew dimmer. This is the “Silence” that the Chorus worships.
- **The Decay:** Like a flower cut from a stem, the world began to slowly wither. Its internal resonance wound down. The Glimmerkin, natural carriers of pure notes, began to fade. The world’s very colours and sounds became less vibrant over the millennia.
- **The Final Echo:** The violent act of cutting sent a shockwave through the world’s own resonance, a psychic scream that planted seeds of Dissonance into sensitive souls.
- **The Prophecy:** Elara’s final wish was imprinted on the Seal, eventually misinterpreted as a prediction of a chosen one.

The Severance is not a lock to be undone; it is a dying body shielding something precious. The cracks in the Seal are the inevitable weakening of that body. The player’s journey is not about “breaking” the Seal, but about deciding what happens when it finally fails – and facing the Hunger that waits outside.
