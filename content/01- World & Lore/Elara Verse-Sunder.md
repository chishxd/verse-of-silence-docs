The central, tragic figure. Not a goddess, not a villain. A brilliant scholar who saw the Hunger and chose to sacrifice her legacy to build a fortress.

- **Title:** The Severed, The Wall, "Queen of Dissonance" (Chorus slur).
- **Motivation:** Protect the world's song from eternal oblivion.
- **Act:** Severed the world, became the Silence. Her consciousness holds the walls.
- **The Final Echo:** A deliberate beacon to awaken potential successors (Dissonants) who might one day finish her work.
- **Current state:** An exhausted memory humming a single note at the edge of the world. She tells the player the truth in Act III.
