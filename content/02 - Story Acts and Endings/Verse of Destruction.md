This ending is the culmination of the pure Power path. The player has killed the Pillars, torn their Echoes out, and consumed them. The hole in their chest is a gaping void. The Demon of Sunder has been their constant companion, whispering promises of ultimate power. The world is grey, the music is a distorted, screaming drone. NPCs have fled or died. There is only hunger.

In the final moments, as the seal breaks, the Demon of Sunder makes its move. It reveals its true nature: a parasite that has been fattening the player for the slaughter. The player has two options:
- **Fight the Demon** and potentially win, becoming the sole, supreme power in the collapsing world.
- **Be consumed** by the Demon, their power stolen, their soul devoured.

Either way, the seal is broken by the sheer, violent dissonance of the conflict. The Verse of Universe floods in, and with it, the **Silence That Hungers**. The Hunger, drawn by the screaming, agonised song of this world, pounces. It devours everything—the player, the Demon, the Pillars’ stolen Echoes, the memories of every life ever lived in this world. There is no return. The world’s final note is a shriek of rage and terror that is swallowed whole.

**Epilogue:** The new universe is born cold and mute. There is no music, no light, no memory. The screen lingers on an empty void for a long, uncomfortable moment. Then the credits roll in silence. The cruelty was for nothing. The cycle resets, empty, waiting for some other note to fill it.
