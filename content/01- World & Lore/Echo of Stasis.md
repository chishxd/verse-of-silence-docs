A degraded fragment of Elara's original severing power, now wielded by the Chorus of Order. It can:
- Maintain the Silence (slowing the decay).
- Silence magic and Dissonants.
- Permanently kill a Dissonant by severing their Echo‑binding tether (Final Silence).

Overuse poisons the world, muting natural resonance and accelerating the very decay it is meant to prevent.
