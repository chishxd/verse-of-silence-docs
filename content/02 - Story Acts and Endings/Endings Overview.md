All three endings lead to the breaking of the seal and the world's dissolution. What changes is the **quality of the final song**.

- **Verse of Harmony** – Wisdom path. The player gathers all freed voices and conducts a peaceful dissolution. The song joins the Verse.
- **Verse of Destruction** – Pure Power path. The Demon betrays; the world is consumed by the Hunger in agony.
- **Verse of Atonement** – Redemption path. The player, broken by Power but redeemed, sacrifices themselves to protect the Silent Child, leaving a legacy of hope.
