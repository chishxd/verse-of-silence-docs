Individuals awakened by the Final Echo. They carry a fragment of the outer song – a single, obsessive note lodged in their soul. The note is a wound, a longing for something they can't name.

**Spectrum:**
- **Revolutionaries** – Believe they are freedom fighters, working to reassemble the "Sundered Verses".
- **Scholars** – Preserve pre-Severance knowledge.
- **Mad** – Driven insane by the note; many are hollow, wandering husks.
- **Pillars of Dissonance** – Ancient, deafened warlords who hoarded Echoes.
- **The Saint of Echoes** – A failed liberator who now teaches.
- **The Demon of Sunder** – A parasitic Dissonant who feeds on the power-hungry.

The player is a newly awakened Dissonant.
