**Available on:** Pure Power path (Genocide-like). No redemption.

The Demon of Sunder betrays the player at the final moment. Whether the player defeats it or is consumed, the seal breaks in rage and hunger. The Hunger pours in and devours the world – every memory, every soul. The world ends in a silent scream. No note survives. The next universe is cold, mute, and empty.
