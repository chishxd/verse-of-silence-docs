The bubble that surrounds the world, created by Elara's Severance. It is a closed loop of self-referential echoes, sustained by Elara's exhausted will. It keeps the Hunger out, but also isolates the world from the life-giving Verse of Universe. The Silence is slowly decaying; cracks are forming. The world will end when it breaks.
