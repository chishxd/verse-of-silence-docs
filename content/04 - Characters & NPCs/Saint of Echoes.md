The Saint of Echoes is a gentle, wounded Dissonant who appears only on the Harmony path. They are the teacher of **Resonant Mirroring**, but they are also a walking reminder that even the best intentions can break against the weight of ancient pain.

## Appearance & Manner
The Saint lives in a hidden shrine — a quiet, sunlit place overgrown with moss and humming with faint, peaceful resonance. They are physically fragile, their body bearing a visible scar across the chest where their own soul was fractured. Their voice is soft, often pausing as if listening to something far away. They brew terrible tea from weeds and laugh quietly at their own failures. They are not a wise old sage; they are a wounded person who still chooses hope.

## The Failed Liberation
Long ago, the Saint attempted to free a Pillar using Resonant Mirroring. They entered the Inner Sanctum, fought the Discordant Aspects, restored the colours, and reached the Silenced Heart. But when they tried to play the Pillar’s pure note back, the Pillar’s pain was too vast. The resonance feedback shattered part of the Saint’s own soul, fusing a splinter of the Pillar’s agony into their being. They survived, but they can never enter another Sanctum. Their own resonance is now too unstable; it would collapse the space and kill them both.

Now they teach. They are patient, self‑deprecating, and profoundly empathetic. They do not push; they simply wait for someone empty enough and gentle enough to try what they could not finish.

## Role in the Redemption Path
In the **Verse of Atonement** ending, the Saint appears one final time. Knowing the player’s soul is too scarred for Mirroring, they instead perform **Soul Weaving** — a fatal rite that merges their own remaining essence with the player’s, forging a **Legacy Echo** for the Silent Child. The Saint dies, but their long wait for a successor is finally over.
