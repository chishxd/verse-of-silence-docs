# Glimmerkin

Small, crystalline, non‑aggressive creatures that hum a soft, sad tune. Found in most biomes. They carry a single pure note of the world’s internal resonance.

**Behaviour:** Float idly. Do not attack. If the player approaches peacefully or uses non‑violent resonance, a Glimmerkin may shed a Water shard or reveal a hidden path. Killing one drops a tainted Fire shard and accelerates local decay (desaturation, audio muffling).

**Role in the moral system:** The first Glimmerkin blocking a door is the game’s initial silent choice – solve a puzzle to harmonise or kill it for easy power. This act starts tracking the player’s path.
