Eons ago, the cycle turned to dissolve the world. **Elara Verse-Sunder**, a scholar of the old Order, discovered the Hunger would consume its song. Unable to fight it directly, she performed a ritual that **severed** the world from the Verse of Universe. She wrapped it in a bubble of self-referential Silence, pouring her consciousness into the seal to maintain it. This act saved the song from annihilation but cut the world off from the source of all life, beginning a slow decay.
