# The Veiled Eye

The Veiled Eye is the Pillar of **Envy**. He was never strong enough to protect the one person he loved, and that inadequacy has twisted him into a creature of shadow and obsession.

## The Wound
The Veiled Eye once had a partner — a fellow Dissonant of remarkable grace and power. He loved them deeply, but his own abilities were modest. When the Chorus came, he could not save them. They were captured and silenced, and he was left alive, alone, with nothing but the gnawing knowledge that he was never enough. He began to hoard Echoes from stronger Dissonants, trying to fill the void, but it only deafened him. Now he watches others from the shadows, always comparing, always finding himself lacking.

## The Inner Sanctum
His Sanctum is a green labyrinth of mirrors, each reflecting a distorted version of himself: younger, stronger, crueller. Whispered self‑criticisms fill the air. Discordant Aspects:
- **Aspect of Inadequacy:** A faster, more powerful doppelganger that mimics the player’s moves, always one step ahead.
- **Aspect of Longing:** A beautiful, ghostly figure of the lost partner, always just out of reach, fading when approached.

At the Silenced Heart, the Veiled Eye’s pure note is a soft, yearning melody. When played, the mirrors crack and fall, and the Eye is left standing in an open field of green grass, seeing himself clearly for the first time. He weeps with relief, and either fades into the sunlight or becomes a quiet, self‑accepting wanderer.
