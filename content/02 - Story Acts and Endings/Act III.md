The player reaches the edge of the world (the Observatory or Sundering Site). The Stasis field is thin. They see the bubble and the **Verse of Universe** outside, with the Hunger lurking. They meet **Elara's memory** and learn the truth:

- The Severance, the Hunger, the false prophecy.
- The world will end; the only question is how.

The final path is set. The climactic sequence begins. The seal breaks. The **Final Silence** makes death permanent.
