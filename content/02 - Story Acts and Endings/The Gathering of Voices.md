The Gathering of Voices is the game’s climactic interactive sequence, occurring on both the **Harmony** and **Redemption** endings. It is not a boss fight. It is a performance, a campfire gathering at the end of everything, directly inspired by the finale of *Outer Wilds*.

As the seal breaks and the Verse of Universe floods in, every soul the player has touched gathers at the edge of the world:
- The Pillars they freed, now empty and peaceful, humming their pure notes.
- The Saint of Echoes (Harmony) or their echo (Redemption).
- Revolutionaries who have learned the truth.
- Defectors from the Chorus.
- The Glimmerkin, adding their crystalline harmonies.
- The Silent Child (Redemption path).
- Elara’s memory, sitting at the centre, humming her one, exhausted note.

The player controls the sequence. They can choose which voice joins next, building a chord from the individual notes of every life they touched. The music swells dynamically based on the order and timing of the player’s choices. There is no fail state, only variations in the emotional texture of the final song.

The visual design mirrors the Inner Sanctums in reverse: the world, which was once monochrome and decaying, blooms into a final, brilliant cascade of colour as each voice joins. The bubble’s surface becomes a canvas of light. When the last note is added, Elara’s memory releases her vigil, and the bubble bursts. The notes scatter into the Verse, carrying the memory of this tiny world into the next cycle.

This sequence is the emotional payoff of the entire game. It is designed to make the player feel the weight of every choice, every spared life, every freed soul.
