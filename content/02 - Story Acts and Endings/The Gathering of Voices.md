On Harmony and Redemption paths, the final sequence is an interactive performance inspired by *Outer Wilds*.

All freed allies – Pillars, the Saint (or their echo), Revolutionaries, Glimmerkin – gather at the edge of the breaking world. The player chooses the order of voices, builds the chord, and conducts the world's epitaph. No combat; pure emotional resolution. The music swells, the bubble bursts, and the notes scatter into infinity.
