# The Hollow King

The Hollow King is the Pillar of **Apathy**. Once a noble scholar who loved a Glimmerkin, he now sits on a ruined throne in a drowned cathedral, feeling nothing. He is the first Pillar most players encounter on the Harmony path, and his tragedy sets the tone for all the others.

## The Wound
Centuries ago, the King was a gentle researcher of resonance, accompanied by a single Glimmerkin whose song was his constant comfort. In a moment of desperate need — perhaps to save himself from a Chorus Inquisitor — he consumed the Glimmerkin’s Echo to gain the power to survive. The act killed his companion and deafened him to the outer Verse. The grief was so overwhelming that his soul simply… shut down. He felt nothing afterward. Not rage, not sorrow, not love. Only the hollow, grey silence.

## The Inner Sanctum
His Inner Sanctum is a vast, grey cathedral submerged in still water. Sound is muffled. The player explores dusty pews and flooded crypts, finding memory fragments of a warm, crystalline light (the Glimmerkin) slowly fading to grey. The Discordant Aspects are:
- **Aspect of Rage:** A burning, distorted version of the King in his prime, screaming at his own weakness.
- **Aspect of Regret:** A weeping, ghostly Glimmerkin that endlessly replays the moment of its death.

When the player reaches the Silenced Heart, they find the King’s original note — a soft, silver hum — buried under a shell of silence. Playing it back causes the King to weep for the first time in centuries. He either dissolves into a gentle rain of light or becomes a quiet, wandering ally who occasionally hums a lullaby.

## Role in the Story
The Hollow King is the introduction to the concept that the Pillars are not evil, but broken. His liberation (or murder) is the first major moral milestone.
