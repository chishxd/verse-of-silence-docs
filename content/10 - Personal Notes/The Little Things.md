I just went outside in a mild rain, after scorching heat. There was a big thunder too.

That made me remember a reason to be alive. These little things are what humans live for.

Then I remembered a dialogue from Dead Poet's Society: "Engineering, Doctors, Lawyers are all noble pursuits, but love, art, poetry is what we humans live for."

Then I thought of Attack on Titan. When Zeke asks Armin why the fuck should we be alive, only to carry our species and reproduce? Then Zeke remembers his baseball. When his own parents were thinking of him as failures, his friends were bullying him, Tom was the one who played with him.

---

## What I Want for My Game

I want my game to have these little moments, which will resemble the real world. Make players remember my game whenever they feel the same feeling in real life outside.

I want them to feel like humans in this world. All other people must feel like humans too. So killing them will feel burdening. And if the death is scripted by me, it should fucking break them. Like the death of Sean, Kieran in RDR2. It breaks you because they felt really humane to us.

I am going to kill people. It will break my heart. It will break OUR heart. But we need to make life so significant that they will actually engrave ideologies into brains.

But aside from death, I want to make this world beautiful. I am going to design some things really memorable, which will resonate with people even after the game is over, in real life.
