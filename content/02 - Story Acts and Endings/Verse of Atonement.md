**Available on:** Redemption path only. The player must have walked the Power path deeply, then surrendered to the **Discordant Hunger** (the inner void).

**Sequence:**
1. The Demon of Sunder attacks, trying to reclaim the player's stolen power.
2. The player defeats it with the help of echoes of the slain, but is branded with the **Mark of Dissonance** – a fatal curse.
3. The **Saint of Echoes** arrives and, with the last of their life, performs **Soul Weaving**. The player's knowledge, regret, and will are forged into a **Legacy Echo**.
4. The player dies; the Legacy Echo imprints on the **Silent Child**, who becomes the new Saint.
5. The child conducts the final song, carrying the player's note. The world ends, but the next universe begins with a seed of courage.

> *"...but it refused."*
