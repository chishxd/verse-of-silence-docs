Also I now understand some more different concepts like Composition and Inheritance now.. Like I had it implemented, but did not know what proper nouns we use.
So Composition is creating components which can be put on different nodes, it's like creating common stuff that many things will have, like both players and enemy attack and have health, so we create `HealthComponent` and `AttackComponent` but then there could be a tree, which will have Health(to be broken, like in Minecraft) but it does not attack
Inheritance won't allow me to make this condition work properly. As Godot's limitations would force me to make even a tree a CharacterBody like player and enemy

So it's not like inheritance should always be avoided, I still have LOTS of inheritance.. I have a `State`, which is inherited by `PlayerState` and all states a player could have, like run, walk, dodge, attack, idle, death(maybe not death, death would be common for all enemies too, I don't wanna repeat it everywhere) all inherit `PlayerState` 
Now some would ask why PlayerState and not State directly like many other YT tutorials?
So the answer is.. I wanted helpers too, these helpers should be accessible to all states of a single player, while still keeping it encapsuled only for Player.. Maybe I will make it more generic, for little mobs and enemies like guards, who will also have same movement and everything, only their movement will be guided by AI.
Well it's thing of future, for now. I am very happy I could learn so much in just an hour lol
