# The Dissonants

Dissonants are not a unified faction with a central command. They are a **phenomenon** – a diverse population of individuals scattered across the world, bound only by a shared experience: the awakening. They are the setting’s “criminals,” “pirates,” “heretics,” and “revolutionaries” all at once, depending on whom you ask.

## The Nature of Dissonance

When the Final Echo washed over the world, it did not affect everyone equally. For most, it was a fleeting moment of trauma, immediately repressed. For a rare few, however, the Echo resonated with something deep in their soul. A single, perfect, maddeningly incomplete note – a fragment of the outer Verse – lodged itself in their being. This is the **Dissonant Note**.

The Note is a wound, but it is also a gift. It grants:
- **Perception:** The ability to see and hear Echoes that ordinary people cannot.
- **Weaving:** The capacity to pluck those Echoes from the environment and combine them into spells.
- **Longing:** An unquenchable, often painful yearning for something the Dissonant cannot name – the lost music of the Verse.

This longing is the defining trait of a Dissonant. It drives them. Some seek to understand it. Some seek to drown it in power. Some are broken by it.

## The Chorus’s Classification

The Chorus of Order brands all Dissonants as enemies of the state, regardless of their individual behaviour. This is a deliberate political strategy: by lumping peaceful scholars with murderous warlords, the Chorus dehumanises the entire group and justifies its own brutal suppression. Common Chorus propaganda labels Dissonants as “agents of chaos,” “echo‑thieves,” or simply “the discordant.”

## The Internal Spectrum

Dissonants are not a monolith. They represent every possible human response to trauma, power, and persecution:

1. **The Revolutionaries**  
   Idealists who believe the “Sundered Verses” must be reassembled to free the world. They are the player’s first allies, providing shelter, training, and the initial framing of the quest. Their narrative is compelling, but based on a corrupted version of history. Many are genuinely good people; some are merely using the cause as an outlet for their rage.

2. **The Scholars**  
   Hermits, librarians, and secret keepers who seek only to understand. They preserve fragments of pre‑Severance lore, often at great personal risk. They are the source of the cryptic clues that begin to crack the Revolutionaries’ story.

3. **The Mad**  
   Dissonants whose Note has shattered their minds. Some are catatonic, endlessly humming. Others are feral, wandering the wastes as dangerous, hollow‑eyed husks. These are the common “monster” mobs of the game. Their existence is a constant, tragic warning of what happens when the longing is not tempered by will or support.

4. **The Pillars of Dissonance**  
   The ancient warlords. So powerful that they are a category unto themselves. See the dedicated note.

5. **The Demon of Sunder**  
   A unique entity – a Dissonant who learned to feed on the Notes and Echoes of others. A predator within the spectrum.

6. **The Saint of Echoes**  
   The gentle counterpoint to the Demon. A Dissonant who sought to heal rather than consume, and was broken for it.

## The “Great Era of Dissonance”

In Chorus propaganda, the Great Era of Dissonance is the current age of chaos and heresy. In reality, it is simply the era during which Elara’s beacon has been working, awakening Dissonants in ever‑greater numbers as the Seal weakens. The influx of new Dissonants is a symptom of the approaching end, not its cause.

The player character is a newly awakened Dissonant, starting with nothing but the Note and a will to survive.
