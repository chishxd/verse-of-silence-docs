Bosses are **close‑range, rhythm‑based encounters**. They are not bullet hells. The player stays near the enemy, learns attack patterns (tells and sequences), dodges through attacks with i‑frames, and punishes openings.

## Two Encounter Types

**1. Pillar Fights – Inner Sanctum (Harmony Path)**
- Entered via [[Resonant Mirroring]].
- Monochrome emotional landscapes that bloom with colour as [[Discordant Aspects]] are defeated.
- Final phase is non‑violent: reconstruct and play back the Pillar’s pure note.

**2. Pillar Fights – Direct Combat (Power Path)**
- Brute‑force battles against the Pillar in the physical world.
- No memory motes, no pacification. Kill and tear out the Echo.

## Memory Motes (Harmony only)
During boss openings, small glowing fragments of the Pillar’s original note appear. Collecting them fills a **Resonance Bar**. When full, the player can trigger the final pacification sequence.

## General Boss Patterns
- Attacks have clear **tells** (e.g., arm raise, glow, sound cue) and **recovery windows**.
- Players must use dash/roll i‑frames to avoid damage and position for counter‑attacks or mote collection.
- Spells are mostly melee/zone; only a few are slow projectiles. The player cannot kite.

## First Prototype Boss
A simplified Warden construct. Three attacks (slam, charge, slow AoE circle). Memory motes spawn after each attack (Harmony mode). Defeat by collecting 5 motes (pacify) or depleting health (kill). This proves the pattern‑learning loop.
