A totalitarian religious order that rose after the Severance. They discovered the **Echo of Stasis**, a degraded remnant of Elara's power, and used it to stabilize the world. They believe:

- The First Verse was a perfect harmony.
- Elara shattered it (a lie they propagate).
- The Silence is a sacred mercy.
- Dissonants are dangerous heretics.

They are not entirely wrong: the outer Verse holds the Hunger. But their methods – execution, propaganda, reprogramming Elara's Wardens – have made them jailers. The Stasis field is slowly poisoning the world.
