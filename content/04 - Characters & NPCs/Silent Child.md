The Silent Child is a young Dissonant, barely more than a whisper in the world. They are not a chosen one; they are not a prophecy child. They are simply a soul so gentle, so traumatised, that they have never taken an Echo by force. This emptiness makes them a perfect vessel for the final note of the Redemption path.

## Nature & Appearance
The child is small, wide‑eyed, and mute. They communicate through gestures, hesitant touches, and the rare, flickering hum of their inner note. They are found in a quiet, hidden place — perhaps a ruined library, a forgotten garden — where they have been hiding from the Chorus and the violence of the world. They are afraid of the player at first, especially if the player walks the Power path.

## Role in the Paths
- **Harmony Path:** The player may encounter the child and protect them. The child joins the final **Gathering of Voices**, adding a delicate, hopeful note to the chord.
- **Power Path:** The player may ignore, threaten, or even harm the child (this pushes them definitively toward the Destruction ending and locks out Redemption).
- **Redemption Path:** This is where the child becomes central. After the player has surrendered to the Discordant Hunger, they seek out the child. Not to use them, but to give. The Saint of Echoes, in their final act, weaves the player’s entire journey — sins, regret, stubborn love — into a **Legacy Echo** that imprints on the child. The child does not become the player; they become the new **Saint**, carrying the hard‑won wisdom forward into the next universe.

## Symbolism
The Silent Child represents the future that can only exist if someone sacrifices the present. They are not powerful. They are not brave. They are simply open. And that openness, combined with the player’s final act of love, is enough to seed a better song.
