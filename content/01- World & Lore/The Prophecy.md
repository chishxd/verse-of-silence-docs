Elara's last coherent thought, etched into the seal:

> "When the seal breaks, may there be one who hears truly, so the end is not a terror but a song."

This was an **encoded instruction manual** and a wish. Over millennia, it was corrupted into the myth of a "chosen child" who would reassemble the First Verse and either save or destroy the world. Both the Chorus and the Dissonants misinterpret it.

There is no chosen one. "Hearing truly" is a state of emptiness and will that anyone can achieve.
