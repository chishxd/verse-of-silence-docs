**Available on:** Harmony (Wisdom) path.

The player has freed the Pillars through empathy, gained allies (the Saint, Revolutionaries, defectors). As the seal breaks, they gather everyone at the edge of the world and **conduct** a final song. Every voice touched joins. The Pillars sing pure notes. Glimmerkin add harmonies. Elara's memory releases the wall with peace.

The world dissolves safely into the Verse of Universe, its song intact – a chord of acceptance and love. The Hunger is bypassed or weakened through unity. The next universe carries this harmony.
