# Act I – The Cry from Silence

The game opens in darkness and dissonance. The player is a newly awakened Dissonant. The moment of awakening is violent: a piercing note, a flash of alien light, an overwhelming sense of loss. Almost immediately, the Chorus of Order is hunting them. Wardens lumber through the streets or the wilds, their resonance‑dampening pulses making the air heavy.

The player is rescued by a cell of **Revolutionaries**. These are desperate, passionate people who have built their lives around a single story: that the world was once perfect, that the Chorus shattered the First Verse to hoard its power, and that a hero named Elara Verse‑Sunder tried to stop them and was silenced. They believe the player can gather the Sundered Echoes and reassemble the First Verse, liberating the song and freeing everyone from the Silence.

The player is sheltered in a hidden base, taught the basics of **Echo‑weaving**, and given a clear goal: seek the Pillars of Dissonance, the ancient warlords who hold the most powerful fragments.

The first biome is a quiet, melancholic place—perhaps a crystal forest or a ruined shrine. Here, the player encounters **Glimmerkin** for the first time: small, crystalline creatures that hum a sad tune. One Glimmerkin blocks a sealed door with its song. The player is faced with an immediate, silent choice:

- **Harmony:** Solve a resonance puzzle to harmonise with the Glimmerkin, pacifying it without harm. The door opens. The world feels a little warmer.
- **Power:** Kill the Glimmerkin. Its song dies in a burst of static, the door opens, and the player gains a tainted Fire shard, immediately boosting their combat potential.

The game does not announce a “path chosen.” It simply records the act.

**If the player kills freely in these early hours**, they attract the attention of a new voice: the **Demon of Sunder**. It introduces itself with a grin, offering praise, easier fights, and quicker power. It begins to guide the player toward more violence, framing it as strength.

**If the player consistently seeks non‑violent solutions**, the world remains open and welcoming. The first Pillar encountered is an impossible wall—a Tree Sentinel‑style boss that cannot be beaten by brute force. The area around it is rich with side paths, hidden lore, and rumours of a hermit called the **Saint of Echoes**. Curious players are gently funneled toward exploration.

By the end of Act I, the player believes they are a hero on a quest to save the world. They have no reason to doubt the Revolutionaries’ story. The Chorus is clearly oppressive. The Wardens are hostile. The Silence feels like a cage. The quest is set.
