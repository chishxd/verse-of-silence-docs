# The Edge of the World

The place where the Stasis field is thinnest. An ancient observatory or the site of Elara’s Severance. Here the sky dissolves into the bubble, and the **Verse of Universe** is visible through the cracks. The player meets **Elara’s memory** and learns the truth.

**Visuals:** Pinholes of blinding, alien light. The distant, distorted shapes of the Hunger pressing against the seal. A vast, quiet chamber where Elara sits humming her single note.

**Function in the game:** The climax of Act III. No combat with Elara; a conversation that recontextualises the entire journey.
