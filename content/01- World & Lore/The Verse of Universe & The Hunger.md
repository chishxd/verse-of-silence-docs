The **Verse of Universe** is the eternal symphony of existence – stars, galaxies, universes in an endless cycle of creation and dissolution. It is not sentient; it simply is.

The **Silence That Hungers** (the Hunger) is a cosmic parasite that lurks at the edges of this cycle. It feeds on dying worlds, consuming their songs before they can rejoin the symphony, erasing them entirely.

Elara discovered the Hunger and acted to save her world.
