# Endings Overview

All paths lead to the breaking of the Seal. The world dissolves. The Hunger howls at the door. What changes is the quality of the final song—the note that this world sends into the next cycle.

- **[[Verse of Harmony]]** – The Wisdom path. The player, having freed the Pillars through empathy, gathers every ally at the edge of the world and conducts a peaceful, beautiful dissolution. The song joins the Verse intact.
- **[[Verse of Destruction]]** – The pure Power path. The Demon of Sunder betrays the player (or is defeated, but the rage remains). The seal is shattered in violence, the Hunger pours in, and the world is consumed in agony. Nothing survives.
- **[[Verse of Atonement]]** – The Redemption path. The player, having walked the Power path but turned back, sacrifices themselves to protect the Silent Child, creating a legacy that seeds the next universe with hope.

The final interactive sequence, **[[The Gathering of Voices]]**, occurs on the Harmony and Redemption paths. The moment of permanent death, **[[The Final Silence]]**, applies to all paths during the climax. A unique scripted moment, **[[But It Refused]]**, occurs in the Atonement ending.
