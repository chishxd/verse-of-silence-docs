# The Betrayer’s Shade

The Betrayer’s Shade is the Pillar of **Shame**. He sold out his own people for power, and the guilt has twisted him into a creature of shadows that cannot bear to be seen.

## The Wound
Once, the Shade was a revolutionary leader, a charismatic figure who rallied Dissonants against the Chorus. But when the Chorus offered him a deal — a trove of Echoes in exchange for his network’s locations — he accepted. Everyone he led was captured or killed. He gained immense power, but the weight of his betrayal immediately crushed him. He fled into the darkness, and there he has stayed, hiding from the accusing eyes of his own memory.

## The Inner Sanctum
His Sanctum is a purple, shifting realm of half‑formed rooms and corridors that reconfigure around the player. Accusing whispers hiss from the walls. Discordant Aspects:
- **Aspect of Betrayal:** A doppelganger of the Shade that stabs the player with shadow‑blades, using the Shade’s own guilt as a weapon.
- **Aspect of the Accused:** A chorus of ghostly figures — the Dissonants he sold — that surround and condemn him.

At the Silenced Heart, the Shade’s pure note is a low, mournful tone. When played, the shadows retreat, and the Shade is revealed as a small, broken man, weeping in the centre of a bare, grey room. He is offered the chance to forgive himself, and either dissolves into a quiet, accepting darkness or emerges as a humbled, gentle figure who dedicates himself to helping the Silent Child in the epilogue.
