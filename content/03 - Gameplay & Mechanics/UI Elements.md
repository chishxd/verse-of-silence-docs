# UI Elements

The user interface should feel diegetic and minimal, supporting the game’s melancholic, musical atmosphere without breaking immersion.

## Weaving Wheel
- **Appearance:** A circular, radial menu that appears around the player when holding the Weave button. The currently held shards are shown as glowing icons, connected by a vibrating thread of light.
- **Function:** Pressing the corresponding shard button (1/2 or LB/RB) toggles which is Lead and which is Echo. The thread colour changes to hint at the resulting spell element. A brief text label (e.g., “Sleeping Mist”) appears only *after* the player has discovered that combination, recorded in the Journal.
- **Visual:** Semi‑transparent, with a subtle pulse that matches the player’s heartbeat. On Power path, the wheel becomes jagged and static‑edged.

## Journal
- **Concept:** An in‑character notebook the MC writes in. It is a physical, worn book, opened with a button (Tab / Select).
- **Contents:**
  - **Quest Log:** Brief, sometimes ambiguous notes about what the player is doing. E.g., *“The Revolutionaries say Elara’s verses are hidden with the warlords. The one they call the Hollow King lives in a drowned cathedral to the east.”*
  - **Discovered Spells:** A list of shard combinations the player has successfully used, drawn as sketches.
  - **Lore Notes:** Fragments of ancient texts, overheard Warden lines, Pillar memories – recorded automatically.
  - **Map Hints:** Small sketches pointing to areas of interest.
- **No quest markers on the HUD.** The Journal is the player’s guide. They must read it to know where to go.

## Resonance Bar (Boss Fights Only)
- **Appearance:** A horizontal bar that appears at the top of the screen during Pillar encounters (Harmony path). It is styled as a vibrating, coloured wave.
- **Function:** Fills as the player collects Memory Motes. When full, the player can trigger the Silenced Heart phase.

## Hole Visual
- The hole in the chest is not a UI element; it is rendered directly on the player character sprite. However, on the Power path, a faint, pulsing aura around the health indicator (if any) could hint at the feedback chance.

## Health / Status
- If a health system is needed (beyond the one‑hit‑kill boss patterns), it could be represented as a series of small, glowing musical notes that dim when hit. No red blood bars. Keep it thematic.

## Minimap
- Optional. A simple, stylised line‑art map in the corner, with major landmarks marked only after the player has visited them or read about them in the Journal.
