# The Final Dirge

The Final Dirge is the Pillar of **Nihilism**. He has glimpsed the truth of the Hunger and concluded that existence is meaningless. He seeks not to rule, but to gather all Echoes and unmake the song of the world entirely, believing that non‑existence is the only peace.

## The Wound
The Dirge was once a scholar, like Elara. He, too, discovered the Hunger, but unlike Elara, he saw no hope. The cosmic parasite is vast; the cycle of dissolution is endless; all songs eventually end. In his despair, he decided that the only mercy was to end the song quickly, before the Hunger could devour it in agony. He began hoarding Echoes not for power, but to have the strength to **unweave** reality, to return the world to the silence that preceded the First Verse.

## The Inner Sanctum
His Sanctum is a black void. Floating debris — fragments of memories, torn pages, shattered instruments — drift in the darkness. Sound is almost completely swallowed, except for a faint, distant hum. Discordant Aspects:
- **Aspect of Despair:** A massive, formless shadow that sucks light and sound from the area, forcing the player to navigate by memory and instinct.
- **Aspect of Oblivion:** A mirror of the player, reflecting their own darkest moments and tempting them to give up.

At the Silenced Heart, the Dirge’s pure note is a single, clear tone that cuts through the void. It is the sound of **hope** — the thing he rejected. When played, the Dirge is forced to face the possibility that he was wrong, that even in the face of the Hunger, a song can still have meaning. He either dissolves into a gentle, accepting silence or becomes a quiet, philosophical ally who speaks in riddles about the value of temporary things.
